# YC Interview Objection Bank & Rapid-Fire Q&A — Evidence Room (07-Interview)

> **Source:** Playbook Section 11 & 12  
> **Interview Style:** Direct, numerical, 1–2 sentence responses. Answer the exact question first before elaborating.

---

## 1. Tough Objections & Core Answers

### "Why isn't this just Notion?"
> *"Notion is an empty canvas where teams write notes; it requires migrating your whole team and still leaves you with a blank page. We don't ask teams to migrate—we model what a Series A startup must have and compile/maintain it inside the Google Drive they already use."*

### "Why won't Google or Microsoft just build this?"
> *"Google sells horizontal productivity infrastructure for millions of organizations. They don't have an opinion on Delaware corporate governance, SOC 2 policy structure, or Series A board registers. Our product is the deterministic operational model and cross-platform readiness logic."*

### "Isn't this just consulting?"
> *"Today we do manual concierge onboarding deliberately to discover what breaks. But over 70% of the core artifact structure is identical across every B2B startup, and we are actively automating the compilation loop into software. The concierge work is our dataset and product specification."*

### "Why would a startup pay before it hurts?"
> *"They don't—and we don't try to sell to them before it hurts. We sell strictly against acute triggers: a Fortune 500 buyer demanding vendor security review, an investor requesting a data room in 48 hours, or a SOC 2 audit kick-off."*

### "What if Vanta expands into general company operations?"
> *"Vanta is deeply specialized in auditor-facing compliance and security workflows. Expanding into general corporate governance, board minutes, and HR registers dilutes their core value proposition. Even if they expand, having the operational graph inside Google Workspace is defensible."*

### "How accurate/safe is the agent? What if it hallucinated a legal policy?"
> *"The agent never unilaterally writes legal assertions or claims compliance. All artifact templates are deterministically generated from codified rulesets, with human-in-the-loop approval required before compiling to Drive. Risky actions are strictly gated."*

## 2. Rapid-Fire 10-Second Drills

1. **How many people have you spoken to this week?**  
   `[Number]` founders. `[Number]` were actively dealing with an enterprise deal or fundraise scramble.
2. **How many are paying?**  
   `[X]` paying customers.
3. **What did the last customer pay?**  
   `$[Amount]` for the concierge installation and 48-hour workspace compilation.
4. **Why did they buy right now?**  
   They had an enterprise customer asking for their SOC 2 / vendor security packet and their Drive was unorganized.
5. **What did they use before?**  
   A messy shared drive, copy-pasted templates from the internet, and a generic Notion folder that had been abandoned for six months.
6. **What is the hardest technical problem?**  
   Mapping unstructured, messy customer documents to canonical required-state nodes without false positives, and maintaining bi-directional sync without disrupting founder edits.
7. **What part is still manual?**  
   The final review of the generated gap report and tailoring company-specific policy variables.
8. **What changed in the product because of a user?**  
   We added strict human-approval gates for all folder movements because founders feared automated scripts misplacing active customer contracts.
9. **What is the narrowest market you can dominate?**  
   Seed to Series A B2B SaaS startups on Google Workspace preparing for enterprise sales or SOC 2.
10. **What is the biggest reason this fails?**  
    If founders treat the initial setup as a one-time event and don't value continuous maintenance (churning on the Guardian).
11. **What number improved this week?**  
    Our time-to-compile missing artifacts dropped from 5 days to under 48 hours.
