## 2. Customer Case Study Verification Checklist

For every pilot claiming success in the YC application:
- [ ] Signed agreement or paid invoice on record.
- [ ] Explicit permission obtained to mention company name or quote founder in YC interview.
- [ ] Quantifiable before-and-after outcome:
  - *Before:* Scrambled for X days / Y hours lost / missing N critical policies.
  - *After:* 31 core operational artifacts installed and indexed in Google Workspace in < 48 hours; passed vendor questionnaire / investor review without delay.
- [ ] Drift caught by Guardian: At least one real event flagged post-install (e.g. unowned policy, modified contract without review).
