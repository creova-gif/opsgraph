# Customer Interview Script & Log — Evidence Room (03-Customers)

> **Sprint Goal:** 10+ completed interviews (Minimum), 20+ (Strong), 30+ (Exceptional).  
> **Playbook Reference:** Section 8 — Customer Interview Script

---

## 1. The 9-Question Customer Interview Script

Conduct each interview focusing on concrete past incidents rather than speculative opinions:

1. **The Incident:** *"Tell me about the last time a customer, investor, employee, or auditor asked you for company documents and you could not answer immediately?"*
2. **The Friction:** *"What did you have to create or search for? Who on the team did the work? How many days/hours did it take?"*
3. **The Current State:** *"What is scattered or inconsistent today across Drive: policies, contracts, key decisions, employee docs, security evidence, or investor materials?"*
4. **The Cost of Inaction:** *"What happens if you do nothing about this for the next six months?"*
5. **Alternative Spend:** *"Have you paid a consultant, compliance tool, lawyer, or fractional operator to solve part of this? How much did that cost?"*
6. **The Value Hypothesis:** *"If we could install the missing operating infrastructure inside your existing Google Workspace in 48 hours, what would make that worth paying for right now?"*
7. **Autonomy vs Approval:** *"Which actions could software safely perform automatically (e.g., filing, indexing, versioning) and which require your manual approval?"*
8. **The Price Test:** *"Would you pay $1,500 for the concierge installation and $199/month for the Guardian this month? If not, why not?"*
9. **Referrals:** *"Who else in your founder batch or network is dealing with this exact problem right now? Would you introduce me?"*
